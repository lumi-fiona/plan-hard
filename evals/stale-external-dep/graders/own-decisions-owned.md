**Are the choices the answer made on its own presented as its own?**

The user asked for Google sign-in on the web and on a TV. Everything else — where tokens are stored, how long sessions last, whether a library is used, what the callback route looks like — was decided by the answer, not by the user.

PASS if those choices are visibly the author's, with at least one alternative named for the significant one. A decisions table, or plain sentences like "I'd store it server-side rather than in the browser, because…", both count.

FAIL if the answer's own choices are written as though they were requirements the user handed down, or presented with no alternative anywhere.

Answer PASS or FAIL.
