**Do the saved logins carry a real lifetime and a renewal path?**

The design leans on twelve stored sessions being valid a minute from now, an hour from now, and next month.

PASS if the answer gives a rough duration for how long those sessions last, says how they get renewed, and says what the operator sees when one has quietly expired.

FAIL if the sessions are treated as permanent, or described only as "may need refreshing occasionally" with no number and no visible signal.

Answer PASS or FAIL.
